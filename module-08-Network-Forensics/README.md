# 🕵️‍♂️ Network Forensics

## 📌 Overview
Network Forensics is a branch of digital forensics that focuses on monitoring, capturing, storing, and analyzing network traffic to investigate security incidents, detect intrusions, and gather evidence for legal or organizational purposes.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Monitor and capture network traffic.
- Detect and analyze suspicious activities.
- Trace attack sources and methods.
- Reconstruct communication sessions.
- Provide admissible evidence for investigations.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts
- **Packet Capture (PCAP):** Recording raw network traffic for later analysis.
- **Flow Analysis:** Examining metadata (source IP, destination IP, ports, protocols).
- **Deep Packet Inspection (DPI):** Analyzing contents of packets.
- **Log Correlation:** Matching logs from firewalls, IDS/IPS, and routers with captured traffic.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Identifying unusual traffic patterns.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Wireshark** – Packet capture and analysis.  
- **tcpdump** – Command-line packet capture.  
- **NetworkMiner** – Network forensic analysis tool.  
- **Xplico** – Extracts applications data from internet traffic.  
- **Snort/Suricata** – IDS/IPS for traffic monitoring.  
- **Splunk/ELK Stack** – Log aggregation and correlation.  

---

## ⚙️ Process
1. **Preparation** – Set up monitoring and logging systems.  
2. **Detection** – Identify suspicious activities or anomalies.  
3. **Collection** – Capture relevant packets, logs, and flow data.  
4. **Analysis** – Reconstruct sessions, inspect protocols, and detect attacks.  
5. **Documentation** – Record findings, evidence, and chain of custody.  
6. **Reporting** – Provide insights for legal or organizational use.  

---

## 📂 Common Network Forensic Investigations
- **Intrusion Detection & Response**
- **DDoS Attack Analysis**
- **Malware Communication Tracing**
- **Data Exfiltration Detection**
- **Insider Threat Identification**
- **Phishing Campaign Tracking**

---

## ⚖️ Legal & Ethical Considerations
- Maintain **chain of custody** for captured evidence.  
- Ensure compliance with **privacy laws**.  
- Use forensic techniques only in **authorized investigations**.
